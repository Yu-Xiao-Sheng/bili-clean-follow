# 行为准则（Code of Conduct）

Everyone participating in this project must abide by the following community guidelines:

1. **尊重**：respect each other. Be kind, constructive, and professional.
2. **包容**：include everyone. Do not discriminate on the basis of race, religion, nationality, gender, disability, or any personal characteristics.
3. **安全**：this community is open to all. Do not harass, threaten, or publicly shame anyone.
4. **反馈**：provide constructive feedback. Focus on improving the work, not attacking the person.
5. **报告**：if you witness or experience harassment, please contact the maintainers.

Violations of this code of conduct may result in removal from the community, including removal of contributions.

This code of conduct is inspired by the Open Source Initiative (OSI) and MIT-licensed community guidelines.
